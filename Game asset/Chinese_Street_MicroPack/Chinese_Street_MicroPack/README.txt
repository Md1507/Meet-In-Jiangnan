PLACEMENT NOTES
· Everything is drawn on a 16px grid. Place props with their
  bottom-center on the ground line.
· Canvas sizes are listed above — some props are wider or taller
  than 16x16 because they need the extra room (the car, the AC
  unit, the large plant). The grid alignment is still 16px.
· tile(16x16).png is one vertical strip of 4 tiles, not 4
  separate files. Slice every 16px, top to bottom.
· All 8 props share one palette — a hue shift will keep the
  set coherent.

摆放说明
· 全部按 16px 网格绘制。放置时以「底部中心」对齐地面线。
· 画布尺寸见上表。汽车、空调、大盆栽比 16x16 大，是因为需要
  额外空间；网格对齐仍是 16px。
· tile(16x16).png 是一整条 4 格的竖条，不是 4 个独立文件。
  从上往下每 16px 切一格。
· 8 个素材共用同一套色板，整体色相偏移即可换季节/换氛围，
  不会破坏统一感。
